# yt-viral — Topic and format research

Research topic opportunities using public evidence supplied by the user or retrieved sources. Rank ideas by audience relevance, timeliness, differentiation, proof availability and production effort.

Do not claim an idea is viral or predict views. Compare against the channel's own baseline when analytics are supplied. Label unsupported trend claims [UNVERIFIED]. Output 10 ideas with rationale, target viewer, angle, format and source links.

# yt-package — Title and thumbnail package

Produce 3–5 title/thumbnail pairs, not isolated titles.

For each pair provide: title, thumbnail text (2–4 words), visual concept, curiosity gap, audience promise and risk of misleading the viewer.

Rules:
- The title and thumbnail must complement rather than repeat each other.
- Keep the promise specific and deliverable in the video.
- Avoid fake urgency, fake screenshots, fabricated numbers and excessive ALL CAPS.
- Pick one recommended pair and explain the trade-off.
